A more reliable Find the Cause.

**Fixed**
- Some apps, Steam among them, redraw constantly only while they are the active app. Hiding other apps could move the focus away from them, and their drop in GPU load was then credited to whichever app was hidden at that moment. Find the Cause now keeps the focus on the app that had it for the whole run

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.
