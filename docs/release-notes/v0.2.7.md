See at a glance whether your Mac is charging.

**New**
- Menu bar: while the Mac charges, a bolt appears inside the battery symbol. A plug means it is on the adapter but not charging, for example because it is held at your charge limit or at 80 % by Optimized Battery Charging
- In the text styles, the bolt or plug sits in front of the battery value

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.
