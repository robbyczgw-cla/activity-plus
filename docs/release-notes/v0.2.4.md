New measurements and a few conveniences, picked from what Mac users ask for most.

**New**
- **Recording sessions**: start a recording before a build, a render or a slowdown (in the window or from the menu bar), see CPU, memory and power over time with the top apps, compare two sessions, export CSV or JSON
- **Performance or efficiency cores** per app, with instructions per cycle, on the CPU page and in the process inspector
- **Neural Engine memory** per app on the Memory page, so Core ML and local models show up
- **Connection quality** (off until you turn it on): latency, jitter and packet loss to your router every 30 seconds, optional public host of your choice, outages in the history
- **Displays** on the GPU page: refresh rate, resolution, HDR, ProMotion, and a warning when a monitor runs below the rate it can do
- **Power adapter** on the Battery page, and an alert when the battery drains although the Mac is plugged in
- **Show Activity+ in the menu bar, the Dock or both**; a click on the Dock icon opens the window
- **Settings export and import** (Settings → General → Backup)

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.
