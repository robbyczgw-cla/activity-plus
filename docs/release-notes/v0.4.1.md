Fixes for the menu bar and the settings window.

- Menu bar items no longer disappear over time. After sleep, with the screen locked or while a full-screen app hid the menu bar, Activity+ could mistake its items for hidden behind the notch and show fewer and fewer of them. It now waits until the menu bar can be seen, checks twice, and tries all items again after waking, unlocking or when another app quits
- The Settings window can be resized, and the Menu Bar tab scrolls, so the notch settings are no longer cut off
- Installing with Homebrew now needs one more line, because Homebrew only loads casks from taps you trust: `brew trust --cask robbyczgw-cla/tap/activity-plus`

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.
