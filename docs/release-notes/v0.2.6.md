See how fast your Mac is charging, and why it isn't.

**New**
- The Battery page shows the charging speed in watts and percent per hour, and when the battery will be full
- Power flow: what the adapter delivers, what the Mac itself uses, what goes into the battery, and what is lost converting the adapter's voltage
- Power adapter: its rating, the voltage and current it agreed on with the Mac, and every voltage a USB-C adapter offers
- This charge: percent and watt-hours gained, average and peak watts, and the charging curve
- Battery health in mAh (now, full, new) and charge cycles against the number the battery is rated for
- Why the battery is not charging: full, held at 80 % by Optimized Battery Charging, your charge limit, battery temperature, or an adapter that cannot keep up with the load. Reasons the battery controller reports without a documented meaning are shown as they are, not guessed
- When charging is slow because the Mac itself uses most of the adapter's power, the page says so
- Menu bar: a battery item can show watts while charging or discharging (+19 W) instead of the percentage. The panel shows the charging speed, the time to full and how much of the adapter is in use
- The history keeps the battery's and the adapter's watts per minute
- `aplus --json` and `aplus mcp` include the charging details, so an AI agent can answer "why is my Mac charging so slowly?"

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.
