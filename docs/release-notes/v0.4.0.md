See where your disk space went, and clear it safely.

**Storage**
- Explore maps your home folder once: the biggest folders as a list and as a treemap, a breakdown by kind (video, images, archives, code, caches …), and a click takes you inside. The map is kept, so it opens instantly next time
- Map another drive or the whole startup disk, and see what grew since the last scan ("Downloads +14 GB")
- System Data explained: the grey bar in Settings → Storage, split into developer data, caches, logs and what macOS manages itself, each with a plain reason and how careful you need to be
- Clean up: one list of things that can go to the Trash, each with its reason. Safe items are ticked, items worth a look are not, and caches of apps that are open are left alone
- Biggest: search the map as you type, with filters like `ext:dmg size:>1gb opened:>1y`
- Duplicates: finds files that exist more than once by comparing their contents, and always keeps one copy
- Storage now sits right under Disk in the sidebar: Disk is speed and drive health, Storage is what takes the space
- Everything goes to the Trash after you confirm; nothing is deleted

**The notch**
- On Macs with a notch, rest the pointer on it to see CPU, memory, GPU, network, temperature, battery and the busiest app
- Short hints drop down when memory gets tight, the Mac gets hot, an app stops responding, the charger is connected or a recording starts. You choose which ones in Settings → Menu Bar

**Why is it slow**
- Crashes per app from the reports macOS keeps, with the reason in plain words
- Spotlight indexing: shown while it runs and what it costs, with a way to rebuild the index (asks for an administrator password first)
- Fan spin-ups and the apps that were busiest just before
- Startup Items marks leftovers of apps you deleted; your own can go to the Trash

**Also new**
- Text size (Small to Extra large) and density (Compact to Spacious) for the main window, the page to open with, and how many minutes the charts show
- Backup status from Time Machine, with an alert after seven days without a backup, and a security check for FileVault, System Integrity Protection, Gatekeeper and the firewall
- Shows which app is using the microphone or camera
- A network quality test with Apple's own tool; it runs only when you click
- Pause and resume a process (after you confirm)
- An ⓘ next to many cards explains what they show

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.
