First public release.

**Highlights**
- Processes grouped by app, with per-app CPU, memory, GPU, disk, network and energy
- A menu bar you build yourself: any number of items, eleven styles, your colors
- "Why is my Mac slow?" in plain words, with a fix for each finding
- 30 days of history, alerts, unusual-activity and memory-leak detection, a weekly report
- Every drive with SMART/NVMe health, CPU and GPU clocks, GPU power, several hundred sensors
- Dev servers by project, connections per app, process inspector, sleep & battery drain
- Automations that ask before they act
- `aplus mcp` for AI agents (read-only)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Notarized by Apple. Tested on Apple silicon.

Beta: per-app volume, freeze detection, accessory batteries.
