Watch your other Macs and servers, and see what slowed your Mac down while you weren't looking.

**Other Macs and servers**
- Share a Mac with your other Macs (Settings → Sharing, off until you turn it on). Pair with a six-digit code; paired Macs can read CPU, memory, disk, network, temperatures and the busiest apps, nothing else, and you can remove them at any time
- Linux machines and servers show up through their Prometheus node_exporter address
- A new Other Macs page shows every machine as a card, with an optional notification when one goes offline
- Meant for your local network or a VPN like Tailscale

**What happened while you weren't looking**
- Slow moments: every stretch of 20 seconds or more with critical memory pressure, heavy swapping, a busy CPU or GPU, heat or a frozen app is kept with its three biggest causes, and marked in History
- Wakes while asleep: how often the Mac woke up at night, why, and which processes were behind it
- Apps macOS stopped, for example for using too much memory, read from the system log (Settings → Performance can switch this off)

**Also new**
- Mac check-up: once a week (or every two weeks, or monthly) a short report of what deserves a look: space that could go to the Trash, apps that keep crashing, a missing backup, security checks that are off. It only reports; nothing is changed
- Wi-Fi around you: networks per channel and band, how crowded your channel is, and advice only when another channel is clearly better. Asks for Location access only when you scan
- iCloud Drive: when it is still syncing or stuck, Why Is It Slow? says so
- Desktop widgets for CPU, memory, GPU, disk and more
- Chips with more than two kinds of cores are shown correctly
- Explore has buttons to show a folder in Finder or move it to the Trash, without right-clicking
- The MCP server has 19 tools now, including the storage map, duplicates, crashes and backup status
- Settings show when the helper is installed but doesn't answer, with a button to reinstall it

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.
