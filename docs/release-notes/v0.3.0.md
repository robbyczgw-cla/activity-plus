Find out why your Mac was slow, even when no single app stands out.

**Why it was slow**
- The history keeps heat, memory pressure and Wi-Fi signal and noise for every minute. A Conditions card under the chart shows them on the same timeline, so "everything was slow at three" lines up with "the Mac was hot and memory was tight"
- When an app freezes, Activity+ records three seconds of its call stacks and the alert names where it was stuck, with a button to the full report (stored on your Mac, the newest 30 are kept)
- Freezes are now noticed after about six seconds. With the window closed, only freezes longer than 20 seconds were caught before

**Storage and drives**
- Space Finder doesn't show: purgeable space and APFS snapshots, such as local Time Machine backups or a macOS update that is downloaded but not installed, explained and linked to the right setting. Activity+ does not delete snapshots
- What wears your SSD: how much is written per day, how many years the drive lasts at that pace according to its own wear counter, and which apps wrote the most in the last 30 days
- A warning on the Disk page and in Why Is It Slow when a USB drive that can do USB 3 runs at USB 2 speed, usually because of the cable

**Menu bar**
- Items can be combined into one. By default this happens on its own when they don't fit next to the notch; if even one combined item is too wide, it shows fewer values. A click on a value opens its tab

**Also new**
- Wi-Fi noise and the gap between signal and noise on the Network page
- An alert when a VPN that was connected stays off for more than a minute (read from the local VPN list, nothing is sent)
- Neural Engine power on Macs that report it

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Notarized by Apple.
